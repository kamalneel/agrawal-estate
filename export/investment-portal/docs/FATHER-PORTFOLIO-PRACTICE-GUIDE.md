| **Invested** | 16,25,000 | Total amount deposited over the years |
| **Current Value** | 31,69,246 | Current balance including accumulated interest |
| **Return** | ~7.1% per year | Government-set interest rate |

### Understanding PPF

PPF is **not a mutual fund** -- it is a government savings scheme with guaranteed returns. There is no risk of losing money. The N-Rank system does not apply to PPF because:
- There is no fund manager
- The interest rate is set by the government (currently ~7.1%)
- Your money is 100% safe

**Think of it like a government bond or a very safe FD.** It is the foundation of your portfolio -- the money you can always count on.

**Real estate comparison:** This is like owning a flat in a government housing society. No builder risk, guaranteed possession, steady (modest) appreciation. You will never make 20% returns, but you will never lose money either.

### Verdict

PPF is your safety net. It has nearly doubled your money over 19 years with zero risk. **Keep it as is.** Every portfolio needs a safe foundation.
