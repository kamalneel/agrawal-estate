# Tax Planning App

## Goal

Provide proactive tax planning strategies to minimize tax liability through timing of income and deductions, retirement contributions, and other tax-efficient strategies.

---

## Features

### Current Features

1. **Tax Projection**
   - Current year tax estimate
   - Marginal tax rate calculation
   - AMT exposure analysis

2. **Optimization Strategies**
   - Retirement contribution recommendations
   - Tax-loss harvesting opportunities
   - Income timing suggestions
   - Deduction bunching

3. **Quarterly Planning**
   - Estimated payment calculator
   - Safe harbor compliance
   - Penalty avoidance

4. **Scenario Analysis**
   - What-if scenarios
   - Strategy comparison
