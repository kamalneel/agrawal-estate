# Modified AGI Cliffs Drive Decisions More Than Brackets Do

Show the distance to every cliff the household is near, and treat an AGI forecast error as a *decision* error, not just a dollar error: a wrong AGI can flip a credit, a phase-out, or a surcharge.

## Why
In 2025 the clean-vehicle credit (7,500, transferred to the dealer) is allowed only if MAGI is under 300,000 MFJ in the purchase year *or* the prior year. 2024 was 489,982, so eligibility rode entirely on 2025. The return's 279,049 kept it; the app's 371,722 would have predicted full recapture on Schedule 2 line 1b. The same AGI decides NIIT (250,000), IRA deductibility (126,000–146,000 active / 236,000–246,000 spouse), child tax credit (400,000), and the CA itemized-deduction limitation (504,411).

## How to apply
- Maintain the cliff table per tax year with the constant, the current forecast AGI, and headroom.
- When a year-end action is being considered (realize a gain, close a position, Roth-convert), state which cliffs it crosses.
- A forecast that is off by more than the smallest headroom is unfit for decisions; say so on the page.
